The problem

A wrong spray is expensive. A late one is worse.

  • Farmers in Bangladesh meet crop disease mostly through a neighbour, a shopkeeper or a call to the 16123 Krishi Call Centre — advice is scarce at the moment it is needed.
  • Generic photo "AI doctors" give one confident label from one image — no weather context, no field context, no reason shown, often in English.
  • Weather apps show rain, not "is it safe to spray tomorrow at 7 am?" or "blast weather is building".
  • Unsafe and banned pesticides still circulate; chatbots happily invent doses and market prices.
Our original idea

তিন-চোখ যাচাই — the Three-Eye Check

① Photo eyeVision LLM with a strict JSON schema: crop, disease, visual evidence, % infected, differential diagnosis.
② Sky eyeOpen-Meteo hours of leaf wetness (RH ≥ 90 % at 15–30 °C), rain, heat — does the weather support this disease type?
③ Satellite eyeSentinel-2 NDVI of the farmer's own drawn field vs. a 300 m neighbour ring — is the stress field-specific or regional?

final = clamp(photo + 0.15·weather + 0.10·satellite) — every factor shown to the farmer, with "call 16123" escalation when eyes disagree.

What is live today

Built, running, testable

  • Disease scan with fusion card + explainability, Bangla TTS read-out.
  • Farm weather: deterministic BMD-threshold risks (thunderstorm, heavy rain, heatwave, cold/fog, disease weather), spray windows, FAO-56 irrigation balance. AI only explains numbers — and we check it cites them.
  • Satellite field health: draw your field on a map → NDVI/NDWI/NDMI time series, haze guard, neighbour benchmark.
  • Krishi Copilot: agentic RAG with tool calls (KB, weather, outbreaks), numbered citations, Bangla voice in/out.
  • Community outbreak map: opt-in, k-anonymous (k = 3) 0.1° grid. MCP server: 5 tools for any AI agent. Offline-first PWA.
Trust by design

Guardrails a farmer can see

  • Pesticide guard — Stockholm-Convention POPs (DDT, endosulfan…) are masked and replaced by a warning; WHO class Ia/Ib substances are flagged.
  • Numeric grounding check — every number in an AI weather sentence must exist in the rule-computed facts.
  • No fabricated market prices; prompt-injection rule in the Copilot; citations validated server-side.
  • Privacy — no photos, IPs or exact GPS stored for the outbreak map; key stays server-side; rate limits per IP.
  • Honest numbers — our small eval is published with its misses (see Whitepaper §7).

Technical whitepaper · v2 · November 2026

Chasha Bhai: Three-Eye verified crop advisory for Bangladesh

Chasha Bhai (চাষা ভাই, "farmer brother") is a Bangla-first web app and API that diagnoses crop problems from a phone photo, then checks that diagnosis against two independent evidence streams — live weather and Sentinel-2 satellite imagery of the farmer's own field — before advising. Every verdict carries its reasons, its sources and an escalation path to the government's 16123 Krishi Call Centre.

4. Weather risk rules (deterministic)

Numbers come from Open-Meteo (ECMWF/GFS/ICON best-match, CC BY 4.0) — never from the LLM. Thresholds follow the Bangladesh Meteorological Department's categories:

RiskRule (simplified)
HeatwaveBMD: mild 36–38 °C, moderate 38–40, severe 40–42, very severe > 42
Cold wave / fogBMD: mild 8–10 °C, moderate 6–8, severe 4–6, very severe < 4; fog from visibility/RH
Heavy rainBMD: heavy 44–88 mm/day, very heavy > 88 mm/day
Thunderstorm / Nor'westerWMO codes 95/96/99 in next 48 h; HIGH with gust ≥ 50 km/h; CAPE proxy
Disease weatherHours with RH ≥ 90 % at 15–30 °C (leaf-wetness proxy) over the last 72 h + next 72 h
Spray windowsNext 48 h daylight hours with no rain (now & +2 h), wind < 15 km/h, 10–32 °C
Irrigation7-day FAO-56 ET₀ minus forecast rain

5. Satellite pipeline

  • STAC search on Element84 Earth Search v1 (sentinel-2-l2a, last 120 days, cloud filter), up to 12 scenes processed in a thread pool.
  • Windowed COG reads of B03, B04, B08, B11 and SCL — only the pixels around the field are downloaded (≈ 3 s per scene).
  • SCL cloud/shadow mask (valid classes 4–7); a scene is usable if ≥ 60 % of field pixels are clear.
  • Neighbour benchmark (original): the same indices for vegetated pixels in a 300 m ring. If the field drops but neighbours do not → field-specific problem; if both drop → regional (season, harvest, weather).
  • Haze guard: if neighbour vegetation collapses below 0.3× its median, the scene is marked suspect, preventing false "stress" alerts.
  • Outputs: NDVI (mean, p10, p90), NDWI, NDMI, trend, canopy class, flags in Bangla/English, PNG thumbnail of the field.

6. Krishi Copilot (agentic RAG)

A curated, bilingual knowledge base of 17 documents (IRRI Rice Knowledge Bank, FAO, WHO, BMD, AIS/16123 and method notes) is indexed with BM25 and light Bangla suffix stripping. The model must answer only from retrieved passages or live tool results and cite [n]; the server drops any citation that does not map to a real source and flags ungrounded answers.

8. Guardrails & privacy

  • API key only in environment / config outside web root; .htaccess denies api/lib, data, configs; HTTPS forced; CORS allow-list; per-IP rate limits (per-minute and daily AI budget).
  • Image validation: MIME allow-list, base64 check, decoded with getimagesizefromstring, ≥ 64 px, ≤ 8 MB.
  • Pesticide guard (Stockholm POPs masked, WHO Ia/Ib flagged), integrated-pest-management-first safety block, PPE and pre-harvest-interval reminders.
  • Outbreak map: only disease label, crop, date and a 0.1° grid cell are stored; cells shown only when ≥ 3 reports (k-anonymity).
  • Disclaimers on every verdict; low confidence, viral disease or > 40 % infection triggers a 16123 escalation.

10. Business model & NRB angle

  • Free for farmers. Sustained by B2B2C licences to NGOs/MFIs/extension programmes, and an evidence API (weather + NDVI trail) for crop-insurance partners.
  • NRB / probashi angle (roadmap): many family farms are financed by remittances. A shareable, privacy-preserving "field health" link would let a relative abroad see satellite trend and alerts — turning remittance into informed investment.
  • No paid product placement in advice: recommendations stay generic (active-ingredient class + IPM), consistent with the safety stance.

11. Scalability & cost

  • PHP API runs on cheap shared hosting; file caches (weather 30 min, advisory 3 h, NDVI 6 h, geocode 7 d) collapse repeated calls.
  • Satellite service is stateless (Docker) — scale horizontally; reads only small COG windows from AWS open data (no egress cost to us).
  • Prompt caching via a static system prompt; client-side image downscale cuts image tokens.
  • Swap file stores for Redis/Postgres when traffic grows; outbreak aggregation is already grid-based.

12. Limitations & roadmap

  • Vision model is general-purpose, not fine-tuned on Bangladeshi field photos — it can confuse similar leaf spots (our eval shows this).
  • 10 m pixels miss small, early patches; clouds in monsoon can block satellite views for weeks.
  • KB is small (17 docs); next: BRRI/BARI fact sheets with permission, upazila officer review.
  • Roadmap: SMS/IVR channel, field-officer verification loop for outbreak reports, probashi share links, Sentinel-1 radar for cloudy months.
